1. Problem Statement & Objectives

The Challenge: Agriculture consumes over 70% of global freshwater. Inefficient irrigation leads to water wastage or crop stress. Our goal is to predict Irrigation Need (Low, Medium, High) based on environmental and soil sensors.

The Dataset: We analyzed a massive dataset of 630,000 records containing 21 features including Soil Moisture, Temperature, Humidity, and Rainfall.

Step 2 Feature Engineering

  • Engineered Interaction Features: Soil Moisture x Temperature.
  • Calculated Irrigation Deficit: Previous Irrigation vs Current Moisture.
